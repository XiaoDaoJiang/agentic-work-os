## Candidate-register rule

A real historical case may be useful without satisfying a protocol trajectory. Keeping these partial cases prevents two opposite errors:

1. discarding adverse evidence because it does not fit the desired model;
2. overstating weak analogies as complete runtime evidence.

Additional candidates are appended here first. They only move into `actual_end_to_end` trajectory evidence after a step-by-step fact pack proves every required transition.
